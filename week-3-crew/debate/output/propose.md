**Argument in Favor of the Motion: Strict Laws to Regulate LLMs**

Ladies and gentlemen, the rapid proliferation of Large Language Models has outpaced our ability to safeguard against their profound risks. Without strict laws, we are blindly unleashing a technology capable of generating convincing misinformation, amplifying harmful biases, violating privacy at scale, and enabling automated fraud and manipulation. Consider the damage: a single maliciously crafted LLM output can sway elections, incite violence, or ruin reputations in seconds. Unlike previous technologies, LLMs operate opaquely—companies hide training data and decision-making, leaving victims with no recourse. Strict regulation is not about stifling innovation; it is about forcing transparency, accountability, and safety standards. We mandate brakes on cars and inspections on food—why should we treat a powerful, autonomous information engine any differently? The absence of laws invites a race to the bottom where profit eclipses human welfare. We must act now, before an irreversible catastrophe forces our hand. Vote for strict laws to regulate LLMs.
